# Spec: migrate outstanding Beads into engineering tickets

## Intent

Stop using Beads as the active tracker. Capture the seven Beads that were still
`open` or `in_progress` as `/to-tickets` vertical slices under this feature
directory, with explicit blocking edges and triage statuses.

## Constraints

- Rules stay data under `control/rules/`.
- No collection without a registered source and confirmed licence / allowed use.
- Point-in-time discipline; LLMs propose only.
- Phase 0/1 only — no deferred computer-use / cloud / rival features.
- Sealed 2025/26 trajectories stay immutable.

## Out of scope

- Re-opening closed Beads or re-implementing completed child work under
  `FPL-bsw` / `FPL-bsw.38`.
- Inventing historical overall ranks without an approved source.
- Re-enabling paid lineup providers without owner approval.
