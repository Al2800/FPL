# 20 — Ownership and rank-aware strategy

Status: needs-triage
Type: task
Track: Phase 5 (competitive intelligence)

Activation gate: Phase 5 must be explicitly authorised; reliable and permitted
population/rank inputs and a risk objective must exist. Captured
`selected_by_percent` alone is ownership, not effective ownership.

## Context

The live GDR currently has no template/differential, effective-ownership or
rank-aware framing. These are useful strategic surfaces but deliberately
optional and deferred by plan §§18–19. They must not contaminate the core
expected-points objective before the Phase 5 activation conditions are met.

## Scope

- Benchmark official `selected_by_percent` as an ownership snapshot and state
  explicitly what it cannot infer about captaincy/effective ownership.
- If permitted population data exists, define and validate an effective-
  ownership approximation with provenance and cutoff timestamps.
- Define owner-approved conservative, balanced and aggressive rank objectives;
  generate alternatives rather than silently changing the balanced default.
- Measure decisions against the expected-points-only baseline.

## Done when

- Every ownership/rank input is registered, cutoff-safe and reproducible.
- The GDR distinguishes raw ownership, estimated effective ownership and
  manager-specific rank risk.
- Rank-aware plans remain optional, rules-valid alternatives and never replace
  the core recommendation without an explicit risk-policy selection.
