# Source profile: football-data.co.uk

- Registry: `football-data-co-uk` (bounded private local assessment enabled).
- Use: match results, fitted Elo and a labelled closing-market comparator.
- Substitution: `football-data.org` can cross-check basic fixtures, results and
  tables after its registration and retention terms are approved. It does not
  replace historical odds or the measured local CSV history.
- Cutoff limit: do not treat odds as pre-deadline unless their own timestamp is
  strictly before the episode cutoff.
- Rights: attribute the source and do not redistribute its CSV files.
- Promotion test: incremental proper-score and decision-utility value over the
  result-only baseline.

See the measured local coverage in `docs/data-sources/wp04/football-data-profile.md`.
