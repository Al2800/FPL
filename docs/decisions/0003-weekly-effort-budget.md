# ADR-0003: Weekly operating-effort budget

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-07-21
**Decides:** the Phase 0 effort-budget deliverable (`docs/plan.md` Section 18, Phase 0)

## Context

The plan names weekly operating burden as a principal abandonment risk (Section 23) and makes the budget a Phase 0 deliverable: it decides how much of the loop must be automated before Gameweek 1.

## Decision

The human loop may cost up to **two hours per Gameweek** at season start, reducing to **one hour** as the routine loop is automated, with the ambition of reducing further — largely out of the routine loop — as later phases earn trust.

## Consequences

- The approval interface must let a normal week be reviewed and approved comfortably inside the budget.
- Automation of the routine loop is prioritised before Gameweek 1; actual hours are recorded per Gameweek in retrospectives.
- A sustained breach of the budget is treated as a defect in the system, not in the human.
