# ADR-0009: Multi-manager live cohort of about five managers

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-07-21
**Decides:** Open Decision 15 (`docs/plan.md` Section 25)

## Context

Section 17.7: a recruited cohort of consenting managers, each a real person running their own single account under an assigned strategy, is the compliant route to parallel live entries and provides live evidence that shadow evaluation cannot (execution effects, adherence behaviour, paired live comparisons).

## Decision

The cohort runs in 2026/27 with a target of **about five managers**. Each participant is one real person with one account and one assigned strategy. The Section 17.7 protocol — informed participation, strategy assignment before Gameweek 1, standardised or recorded starting squads, per-decision adherence logging — is agreed before Gameweek 1. Recruitment is owned by the project owner.

## Consequences

- Recruitment and protocol agreement are pre-season human tasks with a hard Gameweek 1 deadline.
- If fewer than five managers are recruited, the cohort proceeds with fewer arms; it never blocks the core system.
- Cohort results are supporting evidence per Section 17.7; no participant credentials or personal data enter the repository or model context (Section 22.3).
